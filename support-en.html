Quick answers

Which files can I import?
Panel Flow opens comic and manga files in CBZ, ZIP, CBR, CB7, CBT and PDF format.

Is my library stored online?
Not by Panel Flow. Your library, reading progress, preferences and imported files stay on your device. iCloud sync is optional and stays off until you turn it on in Settings; when it is on, your collections, reading progress and metadata are also stored in your own iCloud, while pages and imported files stay on the device.

Are usage analytics on?
No. Usage analytics are off by default. You can turn them on or off in Settings → Privacy.

Do I need an OpenAI account?
No. The OpenAI voice is optional and uses your own API key, which you add in Settings → OpenAI Speech. Reading aloud also works with Apple's built-in speech, without any key.

How do I ask about my data?
Email us. The privacy policy explains what Panel Flow keeps and where it goes.
